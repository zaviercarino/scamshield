Your Privacy Matters.

Effective: Sep. 30 2026

This policy explains what information ScamShield may process, how it is used, and what choices you have when using our website and scam-detection tools.

Information We Collect

When you use ScamShield, you may submit messages, emails, links, or other content for scam analysis. Our website or service providers may also collect basic technical information needed to operate the service.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide scam analysis, operate and maintain our website, and help keep the service secure.

Information Sharing

Information submitted for analysis may be processed by third-party services used to provide ScamShield's features. We recommend avoiding the submission of passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Your Privacy

If you have questions about your information or this policy, please contact the ScamShield team at outreach@scamshieldapp.org

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as ScamShield evolves. Any updates will be posted on this page..
