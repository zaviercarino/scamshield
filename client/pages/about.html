About The Creator

ScamShield is a project I created to help people recognize and avoid online scams through practical tools and clear education. As a student and self-taught developer with a passion for programming, I wanted to build a free resource that makes staying safe online easier for everyone.

From the Scam Library and AI-powered Scam Analyzer to weekly scam alerts and community reporting, every feature is designed to provide reliable, accessible information. ScamShield is an ongoing project, and I'm committed to continuously improving it as online scams continue to evolve.

Knowledge is the Best Defense

Scammers constantly adapt their tactics. The more people understand how scams operate, the harder it becomes for criminals to succeed.
