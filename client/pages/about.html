Our Mission

Every day, scammers exploit trust through fake emails, text messages, online stores, investment schemes, and impersonation scams. ScamShield exists to help people recognize these tactics before they become victims.

About The Creator

ScamShield is a project I created to help people recognize and avoid online scams through practical tools and clear education. As a student and self-taught developer with a passion for programming, I wanted to build a free resource that makes staying safe online easier for everyone.

From the Scam Library and AI-powered Scam Analyzer to weekly scam alerts and community reporting, every feature is designed to provide reliable, accessible information. ScamShield is an ongoing project, and I'm committed to continuously improving it through user feedback and the everchanging landscape of online scams.

General FAQ
What is ScamShield?

ScamShield is for anyone who wants to become better at recognizing scams online, regardless of their technical experience.

Who is ScamShield for?

ScamShield is for anyone who wants to become better at recognizing scams online, regardless of their technical experience.

Is ScamShield free?

Yes! ScamShield is a 100% free of charge service available for everyone.

Why should I trust ScamShield?

ScamShield is built with transparency in mind. We clearly explain what our tools can and cannot do, never store information submitted to the Scam Analyzer, and focus on providing educational guidance rather than claiming to make perfect scam-detection decisions.

Is ScamShield affiliated with a government agency or cybersecurity company?

No. ScamShield is an independent student project and is not affiliated with any government agency, financial institution, or cybersecurity company.

Specific FAQ
Can ScamShield guarantee something is a scam/safe?

No. ScamShield is an educational tool, not a definitive fraud-detection service. Its results should be treated as guidance, and suspicious situations should always be independently verified.

Does ScamShield store my messages?

No. Any information entered into the Scam Analyzer is never stored by ScamShield.

Do I need an account to use the analyzer?

No. ScamShield does not require a login. Begin using the analyzer for free today!

Is my email shared when I sign up for the newsletter?

When ScamShield receives your email, it will not be distributed to any third party services.
